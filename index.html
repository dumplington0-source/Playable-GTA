About

This project is made by a fan to make the GTA series more accessible to the internet. Every panel is clickable and leads to a browser link where you can play the GTA game for free. The only ones without the link are GTA Online, GTA 6, GTA Trilogy, GTA TBoGT and GTA LCS .

Fan-made tribute. Grand Theft Auto is a trademark of Rockstar Games. MADE BY A FAN. FOR THE FANS.
